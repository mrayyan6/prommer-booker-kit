Five angles

  1. AI is the New OS

    CTO Craft · 12 Jun 2026 · Guest essay by Prommer

    Work is shifting from clicking through application interfaces to agents using command-line tools and MCP servers, so technology leaders should prioritise agent governance, agent-addressable systems and shorter vendor contracts.

  2. State of the CIO, 2026: CIOs set the course for AI ROI

    CIO.com · 1 Jun 2026 · Prommer quoted

    CIOs are moving past open-ended AI experimentation, building structures and KPIs that prioritize use cases with measurable business returns, according to the 2026 State of the CIO survey.

  3. Why Enterprises Need a New Playbook for AI Value

    CIO.inc (ISMG) · 1 Jul 2026 · Prommer quoted

    AI value is hard to prove because ROI measurement, adoption and accountability are failing, not the technology; the fix is outcome-based measures and shared ownership.

  4. Enterprises still chase incremental, not transformational, AI gains

    CIO.com · 29 Apr 2026 · Prommer quoted

    Most enterprises still settle for marginal AI productivity gains rather than transformational ones, the piece reports, because process redesign and governance are not yet in place.

  5. Employees are doing the work. AI is getting the credit.

    Business Insider · 13 Jul 2026 · Prommer quoted

    Managers who assume AI drove the work are delaying promotions and trimming raises for the employees who did it, so credit should follow outcomes, not tools.
